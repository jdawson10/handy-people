Handy People

Home help for Taos County.

Small work that does not need a contractor — hanging and assembly, a list of fixes in one visit, seasonal upkeep, and scheduled checks for houses that sit empty.

You get a confirmed arrival window and a message when we are on the way. Work is planned to finish in the afternoon.

Serving Taos County, within about 45 minutes of town.

A visit

We come for a defined list. Photos beforehand help. If the work is in scope, we set a window and work through it in one trip.

Pricing is quoted before we start. Most small jobs begin at a two-hour visit. Repair time beyond a property check is billed separately.

Monthly property check

$150 a month. Month to month.

Two scheduled visits each month, about half an hour each. We walk the house, take photos, and send a short note the same day.

If something needs a licensed trade or a separate work visit, we tell you and set it up. Waiting on site, extra trips, and repair time are billed at the normal visit rate.

This is a check on the house, not a repair plan and not emergency coverage.

What we handle

  • Hanging and assembly (furniture, televisions, shelves, rods)
  • A short list of small fixes in one visit
  • Weatherstripping, screens, simple hardware, small patches
  • Seasonal upkeep within scope (gutters from the ground, screens, filters)
  • Safer-house hardware that does not require a trade license
  • Monthly property checks as described

What we do not

  • Licensed plumbing, electrical, or gas
  • Roofs or structural work
  • Emergency or after-hours dispatch
  • Guest turnovers, cleaning, or property management
  • Moving companies or construction crews

How a visit works

  1. 1

    Send the list and photos, or start a monthly check.

  2. 2

    If it is in scope and in area, we offer a window.

  3. 3

    A $75 hold reserves a one-off visit. Monthly checks bill on the card; no hold.

  4. 4

    We work the packet, send photos, and settle the balance before we leave.

Service area

Taos County. About 45 minutes from Taos. If the address is outside that line, we will say so.
